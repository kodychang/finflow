## Description
Shoko Forms helps you create and manage business documents used in Japanese commercial workflows.

Create quotations, customer order records, delivery notes, invoices, receipts, purchase orders, receiving records, and customer document records on iPhone. Enter partner details, issuer information, line items, tax rates, payment terms, notes, and stamps, then review the PDF preview and save the form. Sharing from the PDF preview screen is included with Pro.

Core data is stored on the device, and the main form workflow works offline. Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription and adds PDF preview sharing plus Google Drive backup for moving data between iOS devices.

Key features:
- Quotes, invoices, delivery notes, receipts, purchase orders, and receiving records
- Customer order records and customer document records
- PDF and image attachments imported from Files, Photos, or external sharing
- Project-based form management with relationship graph views
- Form copy, project reassignment, and saved document organization
- Master data for partners, products, issuers, payment notes, and terms
- Invoice registration number, tax rate, and total amount fields
- Stamp image registration and PDF preview stamping
- Offline storage on iPhone
- Local backup file creation, import, merge, and replace
- Pro PDF preview sharing
- Pro Google Drive backup and sync
- Interface support for Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Nepali, French, and Vietnamese

Shoko Forms is designed for freelancers, small businesses, and field staff who want to keep estimates, invoices, receipts, and related records organized by project instead of spreading information across paper and spreadsheets.

The app includes fields that can help with daily document preparation around Japan's invoice system and electronic bookkeeping workflows. It is a document creation and data management tool, not tax, accounting, or legal advice.
